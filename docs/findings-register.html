Figure 1 — the baseline was in the wrong place

Method correction

Strongly supported mechanism unconfirmed

The symmetric-bracket baseline is ~40%, not 50%

24 / 28 strategies below 50%  ·  median 40.3% n ≥ 20 trades each · BTC / ETH / SOL · 30 days

The method forces stop and target to equal distance so that a coin-flip entry scores 50%, making win rate a clean read on whether a strategy's entries carry direction. Run on one strategy it looked sound. Run on twenty-eight it collapsed: almost everything sits near 40%.

The likely mechanism is ordinary and boring — when a five-minute bar's range touches both the stop and the target, the backtest resolves it as the stop, a standard conservative convention. At intraday volatility that happens constantly, dragging every measurement down by about ten points.

Consequence: absolute distance from 50% means nothing here. Only a strategy's position relative to the observed centre carries information — and that ranking is unchanged whether the shift is an engine convention or a real property of the catalogue.

Retraction

Retracted previously reported as a measured edge

Jester V1's entries are not counter-informative

37.8% vs 40.3% median  ·  ordinary was reported as ~3.2σ below a 50% baseline

Measured against the assumed 50% baseline, V1 looked meaningfully counter-informative and became the rationale for two live bots that fade it. Measured against the real centre, it is two and a half points below median — unremarkable.

The single-strategy probe could not have caught this; only running the screen at scale exposed it. The two bots stay registered and will be judged on forward evidence, which is the point of pre-registration — but the 3.2σ figure should not be cited again, including in the agent handoff document where it still appears.

The middle row is the trap. Agreement among the top five looked like a large gain — until you notice the top five were three variants of the same fade signal. Force genuine diversity, one strategy per family, and the gain inverts.

Every apparent confluence gain traced to correlation, not confirmation. Our strategies do not carry independent directional information for agreement to compound. That makes more independent signals the prerequisite, not more voting logic.

Measured 617 resolved markets

These markets open efficiently — there is no favourite to fade

implied price 0.46 – 0.55  ·  median 0.51 window-start prices across every scored market

The published favourite-longshot bias — a true coin flip trading near 0.57 — is real in the literature but competed away by volume, and these markets are liquid. At the moment we decide, prices sit on the coin flip. There is no mispriced favourite at entry, so that entire family of edge is unavailable to us and we stopped pursuing it.

Corrected instrumentation

Fixed & verified

We were pricing against the wrong reference

Chainlink · not Hyperliquid the market's own text: "not according to other sources or spot markets"

These contracts settle on a Chainlink data stream. Our fair-value model measured distance-to-strike on a different exchange's price — a gap that is negligible at the open and widest near expiry, which is exactly where the model claims its edge. The pricer now reads the settlement feed directly at one tick per second, and falls back cleanly rather than mixing the two sources.

Fixed & verified

Cheap prices with no depth behind them were inflating results

DOGE 0.030 → 0.055 actual real cost of a $5 order, walking the book

Paper trades were filling at the best quoted price regardless of whether any size existed there. On thin books that is fiction: the quote above cost 83% more once the order walked real depth. Every strategy now pays the true walked price, and skips markets too thin to fill at all. Deep books are unaffected.

This one matters retroactively — the asset leaderboard's top performers were concentrated in exactly the thin markets this flattered. Results from before the fix should be read with suspicion.

Method registered multiple-testing correction

The expanded roster now has one frozen familywise gate

57 hypotheses · Holm α = 0.05 strategy × timeframe units · unready rows remain in-family as p=1

Adding strategies without correcting the promotion threshold makes a lucky pass more likely. The new cohort keeps every existing coverage, span, bet, session, effect-size, and cluster-bootstrap requirement, then adds a one-sided cluster-robust p-value and Holm correction across the complete frozen family.

Macro UP/DOWN rows retain their same-tick opposite-side comparator; every other row retains same-tick Always Down. A missing or later strategy cannot silently shrink the family. New hypotheses require a new frozen version.

Operational

The backtest queue is not unmetered

93 runs in ~5 min → account-wide 403 ~2 hour cooldown · previously believed unlimited

A burst of screening calls tripped an account-level block on every endpoint, not just backtests — briefly starving the live signal loggers. It cleared on its own. Catalogue-scale work now runs sequentially with spacing, throttled only on genuine calls, and stops immediately on the first rejection.

Why this register is mostly negative results. Four of the eight entries above are things that turned out not to be true, including one of our own published conclusions. That ratio is the honest output of a study designed around pre-registration and forward evaluation: the discipline's whole job is to convert appealing patterns into discarded hypotheses before anyone acts on them. The findings that survive that process are worth something precisely because so many did not.
